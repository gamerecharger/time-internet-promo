Checking TIME Internet coverage for a condo is easier when you prepare the right details before asking for assistance or submitting an application.

## Prepare your condo details

Have your building name, full address, city, postcode, block or tower name, and unit type ready. If your building has multiple phases or towers, include that detail because coverage can sometimes vary within the same development.

## Ask about installation readiness

Coverage is not only about the area. It also depends on whether the building has the necessary fibre infrastructure and whether your unit can be connected. Ask about installation appointment timing, access requirements, and whether management office approval is needed.

## Use referral assistance carefully

Referral assistance can help collect the right information and guide you through next steps, but it should not require private passwords, banking credentials, or Zoho login details. Keep personal information limited to what is necessary for coverage and application follow-up.
