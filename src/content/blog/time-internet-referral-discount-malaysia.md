## Quick answer

TIME's Hook Up & Earn referral programme states that eligible new TIME Fibre Home subscribers, also called referees, can receive a RM50 one-time bill discount after successful service activation. The existing TIME customer who refers them may receive a RM100 bill discount after the referral is successful.

## What does the new customer need to do?

The new customer should use a valid TIME referral flow, choose the relevant TIME Fibre Home package, and complete the registration process. The referral benefit is only confirmed after the broadband service is successfully installed and activated.

If you stop halfway during registration, TIME's FAQ says you should return to the referral link and start again so the signup remains connected to the referral flow.

## When does the RM50 discount appear?

TIME's home FAQ states that the RM50 referee bill discount may take up to 45 days to appear after successful activation. If other promotions or bill discounts apply first, any excess referral amount may be carried forward according to TIME's referral FAQ.

## Can returning or existing customers use the referral discount?

TIME states that the referee should be a new TIME Fibre Home subscriber. Returning or existing customers may not be eligible, and TIME can decide whether a customer qualifies as new.

## Condo signup checklist

Prepare your full name, contact number, email, condo or building name, full address, tower or block, and any installation access details. For high-rise buildings, exact building details are often more useful than just the city or street name.

## Verify before applying

Promotions, pricing, contract terms, installation readiness, and coverage can change. Before submitting your official application, check TIME's [promotions terms](https://www.time.com.my/terms-and-conditions/promotions), [home FAQ](https://www.time.com.my/support/for-home/faq?faq=home), and [coverage checker](https://www.time.com.my/check-coverage).
