TIME Internet and Unifi can both be good fibre broadband options, but the better choice for condo users often depends on building coverage and installation readiness rather than speed alone.

## Coverage comes first

TIME Internet is commonly associated with high-rise buildings, while Unifi has broad nationwide availability across many landed and high-rise areas. For a condo user, the first question is simple: which provider is already available in your building?

## Compare real household needs

For remote work, streaming, gaming, video calls, and smart home devices, look at upload speed, router placement, latency, and how many people will be online at the same time. A faster advertised plan is only useful if the building setup and home Wi-Fi layout support it well.

## Which should you choose?

If your condo supports TIME and the current plan fits your budget, TIME may be attractive for high-speed fibre in supported buildings. If TIME is not available, Unifi may be the more practical option. Always confirm official pricing, availability, and contract terms directly with the provider.
